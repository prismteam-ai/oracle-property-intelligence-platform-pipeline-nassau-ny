Dear Records Access Officer:

Pursuant to the New York Freedom of Information Law, Public Officers Law §§ 84–90, I request the following records of the Department of Assessment, in electronic form (CSV or Excel) per § 87(5):

1. For every parcel on the current (2026/27) and prior final assessment roll: Section-Block-Lot (parcel ID / PARID), roll year, property class, **year built**, year of last major renovation or effective year built if recorded, number of stories, living area, building style, and exterior wall / roof material codes if recorded (the residential and commercial inventory fields).
2. The **sales file** used by the Department for valuation for **January 1, 2010 to present**: Section-Block-Lot, sale date, sale price, deed book and page (or recording number), and the arm's-length / validity code.
3. Any data dictionary or code tables for the fields above.

I do not request owner telephone numbers, e-mail addresses, or exemption-application personal data; please redact them under § 87(2)(b) rather than withholding records.

If fees will exceed $25, please tell me in advance. Please acknowledge within five business days (§ 89(3)(a)) and give an approximate date for production. If any part is denied, please cite the specific exemption and name the appeals officer.
